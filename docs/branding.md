# Branding

## Visual Identity

Yerevan Dance Festival should feel warm, premium, sensual, energetic, and bachata-focused. The visual language is based on YDF Instagram/social references rather than a generic event template.

Avoid generic SaaS styling, muted corporate dashboards, cold blue palettes, and overly neutral layouts.

## Palette

- Orange: `#FFB000`
- Deep orange: `#FF5E00`
- Pink/red: `#FF005C`
- Magenta: `#FF2D9A`
- Purple: `#A100FF`
- Deep violet: `#2A005F`
- Gold: `#FFD166`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Near black: `#120014`

## Color System

- Primary gradients should move through warm orange, deep orange, pink, and red.
- Secondary gradients can move through magenta, purple, and deep violet.
- Base backgrounds should stay dark, near black, or deep violet so white typography and gradients feel vivid.
- Gold is an accent for premium passes, highlights, small badges, and important emphasis. Do not let gold become the dominant palette.
- Keep contrast high, especially on mobile and over gradient backgrounds.

## Motifs

- Sparkles, soft glows, and curved decorative lines fit the brand.
- Decorative effects should support the content, not reduce readability.
- Use glow and blur sparingly around major hero or section moments.

## Typography

- Use bold white display typography for key festival messages.
- Keep headings confident and compact.
- Body copy should remain readable for international visitors.
- Avoid tiny text over busy gradients.

## UI Rules

- Buttons should feel energetic and tappable, with gradient fills or strong outlined states.
- Cards should use dark/violet surfaces, gradient borders, or warm highlights.
- Avoid nested card-heavy layouts.
- The hero should immediately signal YDF, bachata, warmth, and festival energy.
- Artist cards must handle long descriptions gracefully with excerpts where appropriate.
- Pricing cards may use gold accents for premium or highlighted passes.
- Mobile layouts should be designed first; the festival audience is likely to browse on phones.

## Page-Specific Notes

- Homepage: keep artist previews spacious; link to the full lineup for longer bios.
- Lineup: the full page uses a centered full-lineup poster with accessible percentage-based hit areas and separate stage-spotlight coordinates that open detailed artist bios in a modal. Keep hotspot data editable and poster replacement simple.
- Pricing: make pass names, benefits, and calls to action easy to scan.
- Roadmap/venues route: keep route compatibility while the content is being used as Roadmap.
