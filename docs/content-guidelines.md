# Content Guidelines

## Core Rule

This edition of Yerevan Dance Festival is bachata-only.

Use:

- "Bachata Edition"
- "bachata artists"
- "bachata classes"
- "bachata parties"

Do not use kizomba wording unless it appears in an artist's personal background or broader biography.

## Accuracy

Do not invent:

- Artists
- Prices
- Dates
- Venues
- Schedule items
- Claims about guests, partners, sponsors, or programming

When facts are unknown, keep copy general or mark content as to be announced only if that matches existing project direction.

## Tone

Content should feel:

- Warm
- Premium
- Energetic
- Sensual
- Festival-focused
- Clear for international visitors

Avoid overly corporate phrasing, dense local idioms, and generic SaaS/event-platform language.

## Artist Content

- Full artist descriptions may be long.
- Layouts should support long descriptions on the lineup page.
- Homepage artist previews should use excerpts, not full bios.
- Use CTA links to the full lineup when more context is needed.

## Pricing And Schedule Content

- Do not invent prices, pass benefits, or discounts.
- Do not invent schedule times or venue details.
- Keep pass names and benefits easy to scan.
- Keep schedule labels bachata-focused and understandable.
